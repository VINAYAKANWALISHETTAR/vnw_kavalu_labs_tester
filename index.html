What do we check on your website?

We examine key areas of your website to identify security weaknesses, explain the risks, and recommend practical improvements.

01

AREA 01 / 05

Secure Connections

Check whether your website protects information sent between visitors and your site.

WHAT WE CHECK

  • HTTPS and secure connection behavior
  • Certificate validity and common configuration issues
  • HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects where tested

WHY IT MATTERS

Helps identify connection settings that may expose visitors to avoidable risk.

  • HTTPS behavior
  • Certificate validity
  • HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects
02

AREA 02 / 05

Browser Protection

Review website settings that help browsers protect visitors from common risks.

WHAT WE CHECK

  • Relevant security headers and their values
  • Browser protections configured by the site
  • Missing or inconsistent settings in tested responses

WHY IT MATTERS

Correct settings can add useful layers of protection, depending on the application.

  • Security headers
  • Header values
  • Missing or inconsistent settings
03

AREA 03 / 05

Cookie Security

Check settings that help protect login sessions and keep session information safer.

WHAT WE CHECK

  • Secure and HttpOnly attributes where appropriate
  • SameSite settings for relevant cookies
  • Cookie scope and observed behavior in tested responses

WHY IT MATTERS

Cookie settings help reduce certain ways session information can be exposed or misused.

  • Secure and HttpOnly
  • SameSite settings
  • Cookie scope
04

AREA 04 / 05

Website Redirects

Check whether visitors are sent to the expected destinations when pages redirect.

WHAT WE CHECK

  • HTTP-to-HTTPS redirect behavior
  • Observed redirect destinations and chains
  • Unexpected behavior within the tested paths

WHY IT MATTERS

Unexpected redirects can confuse visitors or create security risks in some circumstances.

  • HTTP-to-HTTPS behavior
  • Redirect destinations
  • Redirect chains
05

AREA 05 / 05

Information Exposure

Look for technical details your website may reveal publicly without needing to.

WHAT WE CHECK

  • Public response headers and server banners
  • Unnecessary technical details in responses
  • Information visible in the agreed test scope

WHY IT MATTERS

Excessive detail can help outsiders learn more about the technology behind a site.

  • Response headers
  • Server banners
  • Technical details in responses

Actual checks depend on the agreed assessment scope and available testing capabilities.

A quick check is a starting point. An assessment goes deeper.

Understand the difference between a basic external check and a structured security assessment.

basic security check

A quick look at selected, publicly visible security settings on a website.

WHAT IT CAN DO

  • Check selected HTTPS and certificate details.
  • Review certain response headers and cookie settings.
  • Highlight potential configuration issues.

BEST FOR

An initial look at common external indicators.

authorized security assessment

A planned review of agreed systems, with testing, evidence, and actionable recommendations.

WHAT IT CAN INCLUDE

  • Testing within the agreed scope.
  • Validation of findings and their potential impact.
  • A detailed report with remediation guidance.
  • Optional retesting after fixes.

BEST FOR

Understanding and addressing security risks in greater depth.

Actual coverage depends on the selected service, agreed scope, and testing performed.
